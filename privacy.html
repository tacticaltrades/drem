Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

DREM is a dream journal and insights app for iPhone. Dreams are personal, so DREM is built to keep them on your device. This policy explains exactly what happens to your information.

  • Your dreams are stored on your iPhone, and optionally in your own private iCloud. We can't see them.
  • Transcription and dream analysis run on your iPhone using Apple's on-device models.
  • We don't collect personal information, use advertising, or track you across apps and websites.
  • We never sell your dreams or use them to train AI models.
  • You can export or delete everything at any time.

What DREM stores

When you use DREM, the app stores on your iPhone: your dreams (text you type or speak), optional voice recordings, tags such as mood, lucid or nightmare, the people, places, themes and emotions found in your dreams, your corrections to them, and your alarm settings. This data is protected by your iPhone's built-in encryption. It is not sent to us.

On-device processing

Speech-to-text uses Apple's Speech framework, and finding people, places and themes uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Both run entirely on your iPhone. Your dreams and recordings are not sent to us or to any AI service.

iCloud backup (optional)

If you turn on iCloud backup, your dreams, tags, found elements and alarm settings are stored in the private iCloud database of your Apple Account so they can be restored on a new or replacement iPhone. This data belongs to your Apple Account and we cannot access it. Apple encrypts it in transit and on its servers; it is not end-to-end encrypted, even with Advanced Data Protection. Voice recordings are not uploaded to iCloud. You can turn backup off at any time in Settings. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Microphone and alarms

DREM asks for microphone access only so you can record dreams, and records only when you start a recording. It asks for alarm permission so dream alarms can ring like your regular alarms. You can change either permission in the iPhone Settings app.

Usage counts

DREM counts simple events, such as "a dream was saved" or "an alarm was set", to learn whether the app helps people keep recording their dreams. These counts never include what you dreamed. Today they stay on your iPhone and are not sent to us. If we start collecting them, we will use a privacy-focused service, the data will not identify you or include dream content, and we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details first.

Purchases

DREM Premium subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, email address or payment details. The app checks with Apple whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or share your data.
  • We don't use your dreams to train AI models.
  • We don't show ads or include advertising or tracking code.
  • We don't require an account or ask for your name or email address.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete a single dream in the journal, or delete everything in Settings → Delete All Data; with backup on, this also removes it from iCloud and your other devices. Deleting the app removes the data on your iPhone. You can also export all of your dreams and recordings at any time in Settings → Export.

Future features

Some future features, such as asking questions across your whole dream history, may need a cloud AI service. If we add one, it will be optional and off until you turn it on. We'll explain what is sent before you do, send only what the feature needs, and require that it is not kept or used for training.

Children

DREM is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal information. Because your data stays on your device or in your own iCloud, you can do all of this directly in the app. For any other privacy question, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If we change how DREM handles your information, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
