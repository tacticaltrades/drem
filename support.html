Support

Need help? Email drem.ios@icloud.com and we'll get back to you.

The alarm doesn't open DREM

Tap Stop or Record on the alarm, then unlock your iPhone. DREM opens ready to record. If alarms don't ring at all, check that DREM is allowed in Settings → DREM → Alarms.

Recording doesn't start

Make sure DREM has microphone access in Settings → DREM → Microphone. You can always type your dream instead.

Patterns aren't showing

Finding people, places and themes needs Apple Intelligence on a supported iPhone. Patterns become more interesting after a few weeks of dreams.

Back up or move to a new iPhone

Turn on iCloud backup in DREM → Settings, and your dreams appear on any iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. Recordings aren't backed up to iCloud; use Settings → Export to save them, and Settings → Import to bring them back.

Restore or cancel Premium

To restore a subscription on a new iPhone, open DREM → Settings → Restore Purchases. To cancel, open Settings → DREM → Manage Subscription, or the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions.

Delete your data

DREM → Settings → Delete All Data removes everything from your iPhone and, if backup is on, from iCloud. See the Privacy Policy for details.
